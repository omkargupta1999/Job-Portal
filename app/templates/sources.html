Add a company board

Many employers host their careers page on Greenhouse or Lever, which offer public, credential-free job APIs. Enter the company's board identifier (or paste its careers URL).

  • Greenhouse: boards.greenhouse.io/token or job-boards.greenhouse.io/token
  • Lever: jobs.lever.co/site (EU accounts: jobs.eu.lever.co/site → “Lever (EU)”)
